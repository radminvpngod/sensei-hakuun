# Sensei Hakuun / Paper Samurai: idea backlog

Gabriel's ideas, parked until their phase comes up. Build order agreed on 2026-11-23:
**Expandable map → Powers & skill tree → Fighting → Mobs & bosses → Story.**

## Map
- **A ring of peaceful towns around the map first**; bosses and mobs come later.
- Sky islands removed in v44; they come back later as a storyline sky town / sky dungeon.
- Shirokumo Castle is sealed until the story opens it. Later: better interior and better door/entry animation.
- Minigames (kyudo, taiko, raking, calligraphy, bonsai, tea, ikebana) and boats come back spread across the map, not all in the starter town.
- Regions to build: Snowy Pass (first, with the onsen), underwater Atlantis, sky town, underground cave town, mountain town.
- Snowy mountain region first (Gabriel's pick). The onsen moves there.
- Sky islands become a storyline area: a sky town or sky dungeon. Fix them up then.
- Underwater maze with loot. Swimming should get harder (breath/current) when we build it.
- Kazahana mornings: during Shōsetsu, flakes drift from the sky islands; catch 7 with the calligraphy brush and Sensei paints a snow scroll.

## Progression
- Possession (of anything, not just koi) is a power, unlocked through the story. Locked in v42.
- No overpowered builds: you pick ONE Stand or power, and fight with combat + that Stand.
- Kaeru's dream: pay in-game currency to build his golf course in the home town. Finishing holes can spawn special items by rarity.
- Every region has its own clothing shop (Zelda style) and unique spots with cool rewards: your first Ghost Stand drops from a boss; Spirit Buddies come from finishing a spirit's questline.
- Gear comes with the combat system.
- Gliding is a story unlock taught by Sensei (locked in v41 behind `store.unlocks.glide`).
- Skill tree on an on-screen button in-game (branches: Spirit, Combat, Movement, Abilities, HP).
- In-game currency: buy better gear, gear upgrades, clothes, Spirit Buddies that follow you, and Ghost Stands (a spirit that fights beside you, Jojo-style).

## First boss: the Red Panda (Nov 23)
- Not a kitsune. A red panda that turns into a massive demon.
- **Setup:** a sketchy red, flaming stranger who looks evil sends you on a mysterious quest. He is secretly the panda's evil spirit, luring you there.
- **Arena:** the fight rewrites the environment into the panda's own dome (like Violet's ult in Deadlock), with things attacking from every direction.
- **After:** you kill it and seal it in your vessel. Talking to Sensei triggers a BotW-style Memory flashback that reveals the stranger was the panda's spirit. It should make you rethink what you missed in the moment.
- Reward: fire, which melts the Snow Temple.
- Goal: an art piece, not a money grab. Style, beauty, a massive map.

## Story and identity (Nov 23)
- **Emotional pillars:** BotW and Wukong. Memories are viewable (a BotW-style memory album). The game needs its own artistic icons (like the Master Sword and Wukong's monkey). Steeze like Kingdom Hearts.
- **Sensei is already dead (a ghost).** His story plays at the start of the game: the "loss you arrive too late for".
- **The vessel:** beautiful. Every sealed spirit cracks your physical seal, and you gradually turn into a vibrant spirit made of many colours (one colour per sealed spirit).
- **Paper identity:** bosses tear through paper; memories unroll as ink-wash scrolls; spirits get painted into the vessel; fire burns paper.
- **Death:** brutal. You burn to a crisp (paper catches, curls and blackens to ash), with an iconic death screen like RDR or GTA's "WASTED".
- **Endings:** the transformation leads to different endings. You become a god who controls the planet, or there's a twist where you become, or always were, the villain who caused the trauma.
- Cracks glow through and turn you into a spirit made of many colours that is always changing. Flashy combat. Opening, look/power and theme approved.
- Music: TBD (options: commissioned composer, licensed indie tracks, AI-assisted drafts plus a human pass).

## Map system (v50, Nov 25)
- The Scroll: an ink-wash map. Cranes + memory reveal: a crane from a high point inks the region as Sensei remembers it, and visiting corrects it.
- Colour floods into a region when its spirit is sealed (hook: store.map.colored). Dev: open the scroll, F2 dev mode on, press C to toggle colour, R to toggle the reveal.
- Gabriel's idea: 2D→3D. Explored and coloured regions pop up as 3D paper dioramas on the scroll (pop-up book). Could also decide the art style: the world starts flat ink and becomes 3D and coloured as you progress.
- Markers to add: Sensei's margin notes, live panda sightings at sunset, weather drawn as ink swirls, today's footstep trail.
- Next: a UI pass on everything (HUD, menus, dialogue, prompts) in the same ink/paper style.
