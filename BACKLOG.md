# Sensei Hakuun / Paper Samurai: idea backlog

Gabriel's ideas, parked until their phase comes up. Build order agreed on 2026-11-23:
**Expandable map → Powers & skill tree → Fighting → Mobs & bosses → Story.**

## Map
- Shirokumo Castle is sealed until the story opens it. Later: better interior and better door/entry animation.
- Minigames (kyudo, taiko, raking, calligraphy, bonsai, tea, ikebana) and boats come back spread across the map, not all in the starter town.
- Regions to build: Snowy Pass (first, with the onsen), underwater Atlantis, sky town, underground cave town, mountain town.
- Snowy mountain region first (Gabriel's pick). The onsen moves there.
- Sky islands become a storyline area: a sky town or sky dungeon. Fix them up then.
- Underwater maze with loot. Swimming should get harder (breath/current) when we build it.
- Kazahana mornings: during Shōsetsu, flakes drift from the sky islands; catch 7 with the calligraphy brush and Sensei paints a snow scroll.

## Progression
- Possession (of anything, not just koi) is a power, unlocked through the story. Locked in v42.
- No overpowered builds: you pick ONE Stand or power, and fight with combat + that Stand.
- Kaeru's dream: pay in-game currency to build his golf course in the home town. Finishing holes can spawn special items by rarity.
- Every region has its own clothing shop (Zelda style) and unique spots with cool rewards: your first Ghost Stand drops from a boss; Spirit Buddies come from finishing a spirit's questline.
- Gear comes with the combat system.
- Gliding is a story unlock taught by Sensei (locked in v41 behind `store.unlocks.glide`).
- Skill tree on an on-screen button in-game (branches: Spirit, Combat, Movement, Abilities, HP).
- In-game currency: buy better gear, gear upgrades, clothes, Spirit Buddies that follow you, and Ghost Stands (a spirit that fights beside you, Jojo-style).
